CLOUD ASCENT 2 — AUDIT IMPROVEMENTS

Run locally from this folder: python3 -m http.server 4190 --bind 127.0.0.1
Open http://127.0.0.1:4190

Changes:
- Noise buffers build in a module worker and transfer to the renderer once.
- Initial raymarch pixel budget: 180,000 desktop / 90,000 constrained-device hint.
- Resolution steps strictly decrease; 44–64 view-march steps depending on quality.
- Slow rendering at the minimum setting switches to the lightweight photographic sky.
- Offscreen/background-tab work and repeated ground-view rendering are limited.
- Camera altitude follows scroll directly; section text, HUD and camera share the same position.
- Broader, flatter storm anvil; firmer cumulus density; darker altostratus; coarser silky cirrus ground fibres.
- Visible near-ground rain for Nimbostratus and Cumulonimbus.
- Separate typical cloud-base ranges from the height/viewpoint in this specific journey.
- Permanent spotted progress with local persistence; consistent overlapping altitude bands.
- Field tools: three-up same-field-of-view comparison and a four-question identification quiz.
- Ground-view text descriptions, dark altimeter backing, keyboard tabs and modal focus handling.
- Reduced motion uses a still sky, instant navigation, no camera turns, no rain or decorative movement.
