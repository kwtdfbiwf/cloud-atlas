OPALESCENT CLOUD STYLE

Uses the existing smooth Blender cloud geometry with a new lightweight surface
shader. Pastel colour washes follow surface normals: aqua on the left, peach and
yellow toward the right, lilac-blue undersides and a pink glow across the body.
Lighting is intentionally stylised and diffuse, with no black shadows, outlines
or faces. The sky is now light periwinkle blue. No new large texture downloads
or volumetric effects were added.

The prior clay renderer and styles are in outputs/before-opalescent-sky.
The Blender mesh source remains clay-cloud-study.blend; the opalescent finish is
implemented in scene3d.js, so no Blender geometry changes are required.
